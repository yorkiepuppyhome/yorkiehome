About Us

Where Luxury Meets Love

Founded in 2009 in the heart of Beverly Hills, Royal Yorkies has established itself as the premier destination for discerning families seeking the finest Yorkshire Terrier companions. Our breeding program combines decades of expertise with an unwavering commitment to ethical practices.

Every puppy in our care receives round-the-clock attention, premium nutrition, early socialization, and comprehensive veterinary care. We don't just breed dogs—we cultivate lifelong family members who bring joy, love, and elegance to their forever homes.

Our Services

White-Glove Services

Beyond breeding, we provide a complete luxury experience for you and your new family member.

Worldwide Shipping

Safe, climate-controlled transport to 150+ countries. Door-to-door delivery with real-time tracking and a dedicated travel nanny.

Health Guarantee

Comprehensive 2-year genetic health guarantee. Full veterinary records, vaccinations, deworming, and microchipping included.

Puppy Training

Optional 4-week intensive training program. Potty training, crate training, basic commands, and socialization before delivery.

Lifetime Support

24/7 breeder support for life. Nutrition guidance, grooming tips, health advice, and behavioral consultations always available.

Travel in Style & Safety

Our puppies travel in airline-approved luxury carriers with climate control, comfortable bedding, and constant supervision. Every journey is insured and tracked in real-time.
